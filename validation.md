# Validation tracker

**The question we're answering:** of the four Submission Kit artifacts, which one actually eats the most time on a recruiter's desk? That answer is the build spec. Nothing gets built until it's clear.

**The ask** (free tool given away, one question attached):

> Free tool, no signup: recruitkit.ezuraarc.com — paste a JD, get Boolean and X-ray strings for LinkedIn, Dice, Monster/Indeed, Google and GitHub.
>
> One question, if you have ten seconds: on a submission, which eats the most time for your recruiters?
> 1. Reformatting the resume into your template
> 2. Filling the skill matrix
> 3. RTR paperwork
> 4. Writing the submission email
> 5. Checking the resume actually fits the JD
>
> Just reply with the numbers in order. Nothing to sign up for.

Send plain text, no attachments. Personalise with the firm's own `/for/<firm>/` page — each already carries a role-matched sample JD and demo video.

---

## Decision gate (end of week)

| Outcome | Action |
|---|---|
| 3+ firms rank the **same** artifact #1 | Build that one artifact only |
| Replies come, rankings scatter | Pain is real, shape unknown — build the cheapest one and ask those firms to try it |
| "We already have tools for this" | Confirms the bigger-firm risk. Shelve; keep the free tool as a lead magnet for services work |
| Under 2 replies from 18 | Channel failure, not product failure. Retry via WhatsApp/LinkedIn before concluding anything about demand |

### Send order

Day 1 — two least-valuable firms first. This is a deliverability test: does it land in spam, do the links render, does the video play in a real inbox. Don't spend good leads proving the channel works.

Day 2 — the remaining four drafts, if nothing broke.

Day 3–5 — the twelve on the tracker, four or five a day.

Night-shift teams reply late. Expect 12–36h. Don't chase inside 48h.
